# Matched Betting Glossary

Load this file if the user seems unfamiliar with terms, or asks for clarification on any of the following.

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Back bet** | A bet placed at a bookmaker that a selection *will* win. The traditional type of bet. |
| **Lay bet** | A bet placed at a betting exchange that a selection *will not* win. You act as the bookmaker. |
| **Exchange** | A platform (e.g. Matchbook, Betfair) where bettors back and lay against each other, rather than against a bookmaker. |
| **Lay stake** | The amount you risk on the exchange when laying a selection. |
| **Liability** | The amount you stand to lose on the exchange if the selection wins: `Lay Stake × (Lay Odds - 1)`. |
| **Qualifying bet** | A real-money bet placed to unlock a free bet or other promotion. Usually results in a small planned loss (the qualifying loss). |
| **Qualifying loss** | The small expected loss from placing a qualifying bet — the cost of unlocking a free bet offer. |
| **Free bet (SNR)** | Stake Not Returned — if the back wins, only the winnings are paid out, not the original token stake. Most bookmaker free bets are SNR. |
| **Free bet (SR)** | Stake Returned — if the back wins, both the winnings and the original stake are returned. Less common. |
| **Risk-free / Money Back** | An offer where your stake is refunded as a free bet if the back loses. Effectively a qualifying bet with a free bet upside on the lay-wins scenario. |
| **Match rating** | `Back Odds ÷ Lay Odds` — measures how closely the back and lay odds align. Higher is better; ≥ 90% is excellent. |
| **Effective match rating** | `(Back Odds ÷ Lay Odds) × (1 - commission)` — adjusts match rating for exchange commission. |
| **Commission** | A percentage fee charged by the exchange on winning lay bets. Matchbook = 0%, Betfair = typically 2%. |
| **SNR** | Stake Not Returned — see Free bet (SNR). |
| **SR** | Stake Returned — see Free bet (SR). |
| **Offer type** | The category of bookmaker promotion: Free Bet, Risk-Free, Qualifying, Enhanced Odds, Reload, Acca Insurance, Bet Builder, etc. |
| **EP (Early Payout)** | An offer where the bookmaker pays out early if a certain condition is met mid-match (e.g. your team goes 2-0 up). Can affect timing of back vs lay settlement. |
| **Lay liquidity** | The volume of money available at a given lay price on the exchange. Low liquidity means your lay stake may not be fully matched. |
| **Free bet conversion rate** | The expected percentage of a free bet's face value you can lock in as guaranteed profit by laying it. Typically ~78%, higher at higher odds. |
| **Bet Builder** | A single bet combining 2+ selections from the same match (also called Same Game Multiple / SGM / Request a Bet). The bookmaker sets the combined odds. Cannot be laid directly on the exchange — you must find an equivalent lay market. |
| **Bet Builder Helper** | An Outplayed tool that maps Bet Builder combinations to their corresponding exchange lay markets. Use it during Explore to determine what to lay on Matchbook. |
| **Correct Score notation** | Exchange format: the first number is always the home team's score (e.g. 1-0 = home win, 0-1 = away win). Critical to get right when laying Correct Score markets. |
| **Same Game Multiple (SGM)** | See Bet Builder — alternate name used by some bookmakers (e.g. Paddy Power, Betfair Sportsbook). |
